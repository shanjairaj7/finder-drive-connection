Finder Drive

Finder Drive is a personal desktop connection that uses rclone to access a Google Drive account from macOS Finder. It is intended for the account owner's private use.

The desktop connection can list, read, upload, update, and remove files in the Google Drive account after the account owner authorizes it. File contents remain in the owner's Google Drive and may be cached on the owner's Mac.

This website contains information only. It does not provide a login or receive Drive files or authorization tokens.
