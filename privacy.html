Finder Drive privacy information

Updated 6 November 2026.

This is a personal Google Drive connection used by its owner with rclone on macOS. It is not a hosted storage service or a service offered to the public.

Google account data

With the owner's permission, rclone accesses Drive file names, folder structure, metadata, and file contents to display the account in Finder and perform the owner's file operations. Read/write permission permits uploading, editing, moving, and deleting files.

Where data goes

The connection runs on the owner's Mac and communicates with Google Drive. OAuth credentials, authorization tokens, cached file contents, and operational logs are stored locally. Uploaded files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account. Google handles those files under the owner's existing Google account settings and Google's privacy policy. This website does not receive Drive data or tokens.

Sharing and retention

The connection itself does not sell account data or intentionally send it to advertising or analytics services. The owner controls Drive sharing and local file operations. Uploaded files remain in Drive until removed by the owner or under the account's existing policies. Cached contents can remain locally until evicted; open files and pending uploads may be retained longer.

Control

The owner can revoke the connection through Google Account security settings and stop rclone locally. Revoking access does not automatically delete files already stored in Drive or local caches. The owner can manage those copies separately.

Use of information received from Google APIs is limited to the personal file-access and storage functions described here, consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Questions or issues can be reported through this repository's issue tracker. Do not include private files, tokens, or credentials in an issue.
